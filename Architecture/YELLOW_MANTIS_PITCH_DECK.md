## Slide 9 — Valuation: Methodology

We value Yellow Mantis using **three lenses** and then apply **realistic economics** (risk and liquidity).

1. **Cost to replicate**  
   What would it cost (time and money) to rebuild the owned IP and capability from scratch? We use development cost per line of code and per system, and time-to-production estimates.

2. **IP asset value**  
   Conservative value of owned IP only (no client work): each major system valued by scope, uniqueness, and stage (production vs beta vs alpha). Already risk-adjusted for pre-revenue and single-team.

3. **Capability premium**  
   The ability to win and deliver complex work repeatedly — full-stack, compliance, quantum, AI — commands a premium over “generic dev shop” multiples.

4. **Realistic adjustments**  
   We apply explicit discounts for: **key-person dependency**, **illiquidity**, **pre-revenue on most IP**, and **market risk** (e.g. quantum adoption timeline). Result: a **realistic valuation range** for a going concern, not a theoretical maximum.
