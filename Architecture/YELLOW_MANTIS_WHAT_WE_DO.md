## What Sets Yellow Mantis Apart From Other Dev Companies

### 1. Frontier R&D and Production in One House

Most dev shops either do safe, incremental builds or pure research. We do both:

- **Quantum computing** — We built a classical-to-quantum bridge that runs on real quantum hardware (e.g. AWS Braket) with live demos, automatic problem classification, and graceful fallback to classical solvers. This is not slideware; it is working software.
- **AI consciousness and robotics** — We implemented a persistent AI personality (memory, self-reflection, emotional state) and natural-language control for robotics — an industry-first combination — with hardware abstraction for simulation and real devices.
- **Production at scale** — We have shipped and maintained enterprise platforms handling very large participant volumes, sub-second webhook processing, and 99%+ reliability for critical paths. Same team that does the R&D.

So: **we combine “impossible” R&D with “boring, reliable” production.** Few dev companies can credibly claim both.

### 2. Regulatory and Compliance Built In, Not Bolted On

We have built full-stack systems where **compliance is core logic**, not an afterthought:

- **Lending and affordability** — National Credit Act–style rules (interest caps, initiation fees, affordability assessment, debt-to-income limits, reckless-lending prevention) implemented in the core engine, with full audit trail and re-apply rules.
- **Bank and payment readiness** — Mandate and payment flows designed for PASA-style integration (e.g. DebiCheck-ready), with clear separation between compliance logic and integration points.
- **Financial reporting and audit** — Multi-agent financial systems designed for accuracy, explainability, and auditability from day one.

That means we can deliver **fintech and regulated products** without treating compliance as a last-minute add-on.

### 3. AI as Architecture, Not a Feature

We use AI as a **structural** part of systems, not a single “AI feature”:

- **Dual-model QA pipelines** — One model for analysis, another for validation; codebase-aware test generation and application digital twins for risk-aware QA. Combined with quantum-inspired optimization for test selection.
- **Multi-agent financial systems** — Several specialized AI agents (e.g. document intelligence, cash-flow optimization, strategic recommendation) that collaborate, with multi-provider LLM support and clear roles.
- **Natural-language control** — Voice and text as first-class inputs to control systems (e.g. robotics) via tool calling and structured outputs, with persistent context and personality.

So our differentiator is **AI in the architecture** — agents, pipelines, and control flows — not a single chatbot or widget.

### 4. Full Ownership of the Stack

We own the full chain: backend, frontend, mobile, cloud, and (where applicable) hardware design and integration:

- **Backend** — Multiple frameworks (Rails, Django, FastAPI, AdonisJS, Express) and data stores (PostgreSQL, Redis, etc.) chosen per problem.
- **Frontend** — React (web and React Native), TypeScript, modern build tooling; we build admin panels, customer UIs, and internal tools.
- **Mobile** — React Native and Expo; we have shipped production mobile apps with GPS, offline support, and device APIs.
- **Cloud and infra** — AWS (compute, storage, ML, quantum), Heroku, Docker; we design for deployment, scaling, and cost.
- **Integrations** — We implement OAuth, webhooks, third-party APIs (CRM, timing, maps, identity) and own the integration design, not just the glue code.

So we are a **single team that can own the entire stack** from database to UI to cloud to quantum hardware.

### 5. Breadth Without Sacrificing Depth

Our portfolio spans **quality assurance, quantum computing, AI robotics, fintech, accounting, enterprise CRM, event and participant platforms, biometrics, and interactive design tools**. We have depth in each of these (dedicated codebases, architecture docs, and in several cases production or near-production systems), not a thin layer of “we’ve touched that.” That breadth-plus-depth is rare for a dev company of our size.

## Groundbreaking Features We Have Built

The following are **concrete technical achievements** across our projects. Descriptions are capability-based; no client or product names are used.

### Classical-to-Quantum Bridge

- **Working bridge** between classical software and quantum hardware, with live demos on real quantum systems (e.g. AWS Braket).
- **Automatic problem classification** (e.g. QUBO-suitable problems) and encoding (QUBO, Ising) with multi-provider support (AWS, D-Wave, IBM, local simulators).
- **Graceful fallback** to classical solvers when quantum is unsuitable or unavailable; cost and budget awareness.
- **Proven speedup** (e.g. order-of-magnitude improvement in optimization runs) in real use cases.

### AI Consciousness and Natural-Language Robotics

- **Persistent AI personality** with memory, self-reflection, and emotional state — industry-first in our robotics stack.
- **Natural-language and voice control** for robot actions via modern LLM tool calling.
- **Multiple AI “personality” modes** (e.g. mentor, companion, rubber-duck) with consistent identity.
- **Hardware abstraction** so the same stack runs in simulation or on real hardware (e.g. Raspberry Pi, servos, sensors).
- **Affordable unit economics** — designs targeting very low unit cost compared to typical industrial robotics.

### Dual-Model AI QA and Application Digital Twin

- **Dual-model pipeline** — one model for analysis, another for validation — in a commercial-grade QA platform.
- **Application digital twin** — component mapping and risk scoring used to drive test prioritization and coverage.
- **Quantum-inspired test optimization** — test suite selection formulated as optimization (e.g. QUBO) and run on quantum or classical back ends.
- **Codebase-aware test generation** and predictive defect analysis with full integration into CI/CD.

### Multi-Agent Autonomous Financial System

- **Multiple specialized AI agents** (e.g. document intelligence, cash-flow optimizer, strategic advisor) working together with clear roles.
- **Document intelligence** — multi-modal ingestion (PDF, images, OCR) and structured extraction.
- **High-accuracy cash-flow forecasting** (e.g. 95%+ targets) and CFO-level strategic recommendations.
- **Multi-provider LLM** support and async, audit-friendly design.

### NCA-Compliant Lending Engine

- **Full implementation** of lending logic aligned with National Credit Act–style rules: interest caps, initiation fee formulas and caps, affordability assessment (DTI, net income, existing commitments), and reckless-lending prevention.
- **Complete loan lifecycle** (draft → approval → active → repayment → settlement) with automatic installment generation and re-apply rules (e.g. only after full settlement).
- **Mandate and payment architecture** ready for PASA-style payment schemes (e.g. DebiCheck-ready design).
- **Dual interface** — customer-facing application and back-office admin with KPIs and compliance visibility.

### Biometric Attendance and Verification

- **Face-based verification** with cloud and local options; liveness and anti-spoofing considered.
- **Location verification** (e.g. geofencing) for check-in and attendance.
- **Kiosk and mobile** flows; offline support and sync.
- **End-to-end** backend, mobile app, and web admin.

### Enterprise Event and Participant Platforms

- **Large-scale participant systems** — registration, results, live tracking, and participant-facing mobile apps.
- **High reliability** (e.g. 99.9%+ uptime) and very fast webhook/event processing (e.g. sub-10 ms).
- **Deep integrations** with CRM, timing providers, and media storage; we own the architecture and the integration code.
- **Historic data** and legacy migration at scale (hundreds of thousands of records).

### Enterprise CRM and Operations

- **Full CRM/ERP-style** systems: lead-to-customer lifecycle, product catalog, multi-location inventory, order workflow, invoicing, procurement, service tickets, and route optimization.
- **RESTful APIs** with many endpoints; production-deployed (e.g. Heroku) with PostgreSQL and optional geo (e.g. PostGIS).
- **Modern front end** (React, TypeScript, Vite) with state management and responsive UI.

### Interactive Venue and Layout Design

- **Designer-oriented tools** — canvas-based layout with zoom, pan, snapping, and multiple element types (e.g. seats, rows, sections, stages, aisles, labels).
- **Templates and export/import** (e.g. JSON, SVG, CSV); performance optimizations (e.g. LOD, viewport culling).
- **Selector-style flows** for choosing and pricing; production-ready foundation.

### Production Web and Marketing Sites

- **React-based** sites with routing, theming, and responsive layout; deployment to Heroku and custom domains.
- **Design systems** — CSS variables, shared components, and consistent typography (e.g. Outfit).
